Privacy, terms & data processing

Preview-stage privacy and use information. Updated 14 November 2026.

Preview notice: Chatora has no paid plan, billing, live messaging channels or AI replies enabled. This page is an informational draft, not a signed DPA or a substitute for legal advice. Do not submit sensitive or regulated data.

Privacy policy

The current Chatora preview may process account data (email and workspace settings), contacts you add or import, and information sent through public forms. Live customer conversations, subscription billing and AI-generated replies are not enabled in this preview.

  • What we collect: account email and authentication data, workspace settings, contact records you add or import, and public-form submissions including update-interest requests. These may include email/name/channel/topic/message plus page, language, referrer, UTM and browser details. Pageview events may also be recorded. If a form is submitted offline, its details may be held in this browser's local storage until sent; avoid shared devices and do not enter sensitive information.
  • Why: to authenticate your account, save workspace changes, handle enquiries or record product-update interest, and understand basic site usage. An update-interest request does not subscribe you to a mailing list; the preview is not used to send advertising profiles.
  • Analytics choice: page views are counted only if you accept the notice shown on your first visit. The counter records the page, its title, the language and the referring address with a random ID per view; it sets no cookies and builds no profile. A Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal from your browser is treated as a refusal. Your choice is kept in this browser's local storage, as is the session token when you sign in, and you can change it below.
  • AI processing: live AI replies and external AI processing are not enabled in this preview. Do not put sensitive information into illustrative flow drafts.
  • Retention: anonymous page-view counters are deleted automatically 90 days after they are recorded. Form submissions are kept until you ask for deletion or the preview ends. Account and workspace data are kept until you delete the account from Settings in the workspace, which deletes the account and its workspace data. Any backups held by the hosting provider expire on that provider's schedule, which this preview does not control. Offline form details may remain in this browser's local storage until they are sent or you clear browser data.
  • Service providers: Supabase (authentication and database; project region eu-central-1, Frankfurt, EU) and Vercel (website hosting; receives standard request data such as IP address and browser details when a page loads). No payment, channel or AI provider is connected in the preview. This list is informational, not a contractual sub-processor schedule.
  • Privacy requests: you can delete your account and its workspace data yourself from Settings in the workspace. For access, correction or deletion of anything else, including form submissions, use the contact page; no response-time commitment is published for this preview.

Terms of service

This section describes current preview use only. It is not a signed commercial services agreement; the terms should be reviewed before any paid or live messaging service is launched.

  • Acceptable use: no spam, no purchased contact lists, no content that violates platform policies or applicable law. You must collect valid opt-ins for each channel you message on.
  • Platform rules: no social messaging provider is connected in the current preview. Any future channel use must follow that provider's current policies and consent requirements.
  • Billing: no Chatora subscription plan, checkout or recurring billing is enabled in this preview. No overage or subscription fee is currently offered.
  • Refunds: there is no paid Chatora plan in the current preview, and no refund guarantee is being offered.
  • Availability: no uptime target, service-level agreement or service credit has been published for this preview.
  • Commercial terms: a reviewed limitation-of-liability clause and warranty terms have not been published for this product preview.
  • Access: you can stop using the preview at any time. Access may be suspended to address abuse, security issues or unlawful use; no paid-plan cancellation process is active.

Data processing addendum

No signed data-processing addendum (DPA) or Standard Contractual Clauses are currently published for this preview. Do not use it to process regulated, sensitive or third-party customer data where a DPA is required.

  • Controller/processor roles have not been set out in a signed agreement.
  • The providers named in the privacy section are the current list; no commitment to give advance notice of changes is published.
  • Independent security certifications, penetration-test results and at-rest encryption specifications have not been verified in this local review.
  • A contractual breach-notification deadline is not published.
  • The live project stores its data in Supabase region eu-central-1 (Frankfurt, EU). No data-residency choice is offered.

No countersigned DPA is available in the current product preview.

Security

The preview does not ask for social-platform passwords, and channel OAuth onboarding is not enabled. This local audit did not verify encryption-at-rest settings, an immutable audit log, security certifications or penetration testing; no security guarantee or bug bounty is offered here.

Questions? Use the contact page for privacy or security questions. Response times, a signed DPA and a paid bug bounty are not offered or guaranteed by this preview.
