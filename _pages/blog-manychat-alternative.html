1. ManyChat's current published plans

The USD pricing page checked on 28 October 2026 lists Free at $0 for 25 contacts; Essential at $14/month equivalent for 250; Pro at $29 for 2,500; Business at $69 for 7,500; and Advanced at $139 for 25,000. The monthly amounts shown are annual-billing equivalents; regional prices, billing terms and taxes may differ.

The plan allowance is only one part of the decision. Confirm the displayed billing interval, the account’s country and the features your workflow requires on ManyChat’s official pricing page.

3. Feature and support trade-offs

Compare the things your team will actually use, not just the headline price:

  • Seats. ManyChat lists user allowances by plan. Check the current plan table before estimating team access; Chatora has not published a paid-seat policy.
  • Channels. ManyChat’s channel availability varies by tier. Chatora’s messaging channels are not connected in the current preview.
  • Arabic. Chatora’s website and workspace preview support Arabic and English interfaces. Live AI replies, dialect matching and Arabic message automation are not enabled.
  • Attribution. Chatora does not currently provide live revenue attribution or connected commerce analytics. Ask each provider to demonstrate the event sources and reporting it supports.
  • Support. Compare the published support channels and response commitments for the specific plan. Chatora has not published service-level or 24/7 support commitments.

4. A safe switching checklist

  1. Choose a quiet window. Avoid the days around a product launch or a major sale.
  2. Export and back up first. Save contacts, flow definitions, tags and message templates using the tools supported by your current provider. Chatora does not offer a tested migration service.
  3. Test before replacing. Validate triggers, permissions, opt-ins and handoff behaviour in a test workspace; avoid sending duplicate messages to the same audience.
  4. Reconnect only after approval. Confirm provider requirements, channel permissions and any required Meta approval before authorizing a production account.
  5. Cancel only after validation. Keep the existing service until exports, channel access and required workflows have been checked.

5. When staying with ManyChat may be better

Switching has a real cost. Staying can make sense when:

  • Your team depends on a community-built template or workflow you have already tested.
  • Your agency relies on ManyChat’s published partner resources or established client workflows.
  • Your staff are trained on the current builder and a change would require retraining or re-approval.
  • You need a live integration or support commitment that another provider has not demonstrated.

There is no universal winner from these plan snapshots. Compare the exact workflow, account region, support needs and total cost before making a decision.

6. FAQ

Is Chatora a live ManyChat alternative?

Not yet. Chatora is a product preview with editable starter drafts, CSV tools and an authenticated workspace. It has no published subscription price, connected messaging channels, live AI replies, migration tool or revenue-attribution service.

Do I lose contacts if I switch tools?

Data access and export depend on the channel and provider. Export and back up your contact records, flow definitions and relevant history first; verify ownership and permissions with the platform before disconnecting an account.

Does Chatora offer a free plan or refund?

Chatora has not published free or paid subscription plans, checkout, or refund terms. The current product preview is not a free subscription offer.

Does Chatora support Arabic AI replies?

The site and workspace preview have Arabic and English interfaces. Live AI replies, dialect detection and channel messaging are not enabled.
