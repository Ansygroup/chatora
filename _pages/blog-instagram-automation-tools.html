1. What Instagram automation can include in 2026

Three distinct jobs get bundled under the label, and tools are rarely strong at all three:

  • Comment automation — replying publicly to comments on posts and Reels, then moving the conversation to DMs when someone asks "price?", "link?", "bkam?".
  • DM automation — keyword flows, quick replies, product cards, checkout links, qualification and follow-ups inside the inbox.
  • Story & mention handling — thank-yous, referral codes, UGC consent requests.

Check which Instagram features each vendor supports through Meta-authorized access and what permissions it needs. Avoid tools that ask you to share your Instagram password or rely on undocumented scraping; verify current platform policies before connecting an account.

3. How to assess performance evidence

A polished demo does not establish reliability under peak traffic. Ask for the limits and test conditions behind any performance claim, then run a small, controlled test within the provider’s rules.

  1. Duplicate events. Ask how repeated webhooks or comments are detected and whether the vendor documents idempotency and retry behavior.
  2. Queue delays. Ask how the system handles bursts, retries and backlogs, and how it reports delayed or failed sends.
  3. API limits. Confirm that the integration uses Meta-authorized access and understand the relevant rate limits, permissions and policy requirements.

Chatora has no connected Instagram channel or production automation in the current preview. Its local draft simulator does not test Meta delivery, throughput, queueing or response time.

4. The buyer's checklist

  • Confirm official API access, required permissions and the provider’s plan for policy changes.
  • Ask how comment triggers, duplicate events, retries and opt-outs are handled; test only in a permitted workspace.
  • Request measured end-to-end latency under a stated load; choose a service target that fits your own needs.
  • For AI features, test whether answers use your approved catalogue and handle unknown prices safely; verify that feature is live on the plan.
  • Check human handoff rules and whether context reaches the receiving agent.
  • If you sell in MENA, test Arabic and dialect use cases with real, approved examples.
  • Compare total cost at your expected volume, including contact or conversation limits, seats, add-ons, usage and taxes.
  • Check data access, export scope and retention before connecting a production account.
