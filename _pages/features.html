Chatora’s product direction is AI-assisted conversation marketing: intent-aware replies, approved product information and human handoff. These are roadmap concepts; the current preview has no live AI or connected messaging channels.

Availability note: this page includes roadmap concepts and illustrative interface examples, not features currently live. The preview supports password-based accounts, editable flow drafts, 45 editable starter drafts with generic placeholder steps, CSV contact tools, local flow tests and broadcast audience previews. The drafts are not production-tested. Live AI, channel integrations, outbound messages, commerce connectors and product analytics are not enabled. Figures and conversations shown are placeholders, not customer results.

02 · Automation

Flow automation: a roadmap concept

A future text prompt could help draft a workflow. The current preview lets you edit saved draft steps manually; it does not generate flows from prompts, activate triggers or execute automation.

  • Planned triggers include comments, DMs, Story mentions, ad clicks, keywords and webhooks; none is connected here
  • 45 editable starter drafts with generic placeholders; not production-tested or ready automations
  • Delay steps can be saved in drafts; timezone-aware quiet-hour rules are not implemented
  • Version history and one-click rollback are not available in the preview

03 · Unified inbox

Unified inbox: a future product direction

A unified inbox is a roadmap concept. The current preview has no live connections for Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok, Messenger, Telegram, SMS, email or web chat.

  • The preview does not combine messages, orders, tags and notes across channels into one customer profile
  • Saved-reply libraries and AI-written drafts are not available; no AI provider is connected
  • Assignment rules, service-level automation and collision detection are not enabled
  • Team seats and plan limits are not published; this preview is scoped to the signed-in account

07 · Trust & security

Verify security controls before relying on them

This preview is not a security certification or service-level agreement. No messaging channels are connected; review the privacy notice before submitting data.

Official API status

The product direction favors documented, approved APIs. No external channel integration or Meta partner approval has been verified for this preview.

Privacy and data residency

A verified hosting-region list, signed DPA, encryption-at-rest specification and security certifications are not published; see the legal notice.

Availability commitments

No 99.99% uptime target, multi-region redundancy or public status page has been verified or promised.

AI data handling

No AI provider is connected and external AI processing is not enabled. Do not submit sensitive data; terms for any future provider are not established here.

Audit logs and SSO

Role-based permissions, immutable audit logs and SSO are not available in this preview; no team-level permission model is advertised.
