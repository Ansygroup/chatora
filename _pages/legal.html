Privacy, terms & data processing

Preview-stage privacy and use information. Updated 28 October 2026.

Preview notice: Chatora has no paid plan, billing, live messaging channels or AI replies enabled. This page is an informational draft, not a signed DPA or a substitute for legal advice. Do not submit sensitive or regulated data.

Privacy policy

The current Chatora preview may process account data (email and workspace settings), contacts you add or import, and information sent through public forms. Live customer conversations, subscription billing and AI-generated replies are not enabled in this preview.

  • What we collect: account email and authentication data, workspace settings, contact records you add or import, and public-form submissions including update-interest requests. These may include email/name/channel/topic/message plus page, language, referrer, UTM and browser details. Pageview events may also be recorded. If a form is submitted offline, its details may be held in this browser's local storage until sent; avoid shared devices and do not enter sensitive information.
  • Why: to authenticate your account, save workspace changes, handle enquiries or record product-update interest, and understand basic site usage. An update-interest request does not subscribe you to a mailing list; the preview is not used to send advertising profiles.
  • AI processing: live AI replies and external AI processing are not enabled in this preview. Do not put sensitive information into illustrative flow drafts.
  • Retention: a public retention period or backup-deletion timeline has not been published for this preview. You can manage records exposed in the workspace; contact the team for account or privacy requests. Offline form details may remain in local storage until submission or manual browser-data deletion.
  • Service providers: the site code is configured to use Supabase for authentication and workspace data. Live hosting region and any complete sub-processor list have not been verified or published here. No payment, channel or AI provider is connected in the preview.
  • Privacy requests: you may request access, correction or deletion where applicable. Use the contact page to submit a request; no response-time commitment is published for this preview.

Terms of service

This section describes current preview use only. It is not a signed commercial services agreement; the terms should be reviewed before any paid or live messaging service is launched.

  • Acceptable use: no spam, no purchased contact lists, no content that violates platform policies or applicable law. You must collect valid opt-ins for each channel you message on.
  • Platform rules: no social messaging provider is connected in the current preview. Any future channel use must follow that provider's current policies and consent requirements.
  • Billing: no Chatora subscription plan, checkout or recurring billing is enabled in this preview. No overage or subscription fee is currently offered.
  • Refunds: there is no paid Chatora plan in the current preview, and no refund guarantee is being offered.
  • Availability: no uptime target, service-level agreement or service credit has been published for this preview.
  • Commercial terms: a reviewed limitation-of-liability clause and warranty terms have not been published for this product preview.
  • Access: you can stop using the preview at any time. Access may be suspended to address abuse, security issues or unlawful use; no paid-plan cancellation process is active.
