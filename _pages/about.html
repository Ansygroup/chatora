What we believe

Five principles we won't trade away

Pricing should never punish growth

Pricing should be transparent and understandable. Chatora has not published subscription prices, contact allowances or team-seat terms; the current page is a preview, not a paid offer.

Official APIs, or nothing

The product direction favors documented, approved APIs over password sharing or scraping. No external messaging channel is connected in the current preview.

Dialects are not a nice-to-have

Arabic and English interfaces are available in the preview. Live AI dialect matching is not enabled; language behavior should be tested before it is promised.

Humans are the feature

The product vision is to use AI for routine assistance and hand over when a person is needed. Live AI replies are not enabled in the current preview.

Be honest in comparisons

Our comparisons should show where established vendors have advantages and disclose Chatora’s preview limits; no savings claim is valid without a published Chatora price.

Product direction

A clearer workspace for conversations

The preview uses Supabase for password-based accounts and account-scoped workspace data; Google sign-in is not configured. Editable flow drafts, contact CSV tools and audience previews are available, but messaging channels, outbound replies, a live AI agent and subscription billing are not enabled.

The product direction combines structured automation with AI-assisted conversations. No customer results, published subscription price, service-level commitment or launch date is claimed here.
