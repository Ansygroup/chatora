Chatora does not automate Instagram in the current preview. No channel provider is connected. Any future integration must use an approved provider and follow the platform’s current policies; no account-safety guarantee is made.
No coding is needed to explore the current draft editor, CSV tools or local flow simulation. AI-generated flows, a public API and webhooks are not available in this preview.
Chatora has not published contact-based plan limits or billing rules. The preview has no subscription billing; see the pricing page for current availability.
The website and workspace interface are available in Arabic and English. Live AI replies are not available in the current preview.
A ManyChat migration tool or assisted migration service is not available. You can review and manually adapt the editable starter drafts in the gallery.
Chatora has not announced free or paid subscription plans. Billing and checkout are not enabled in the current product preview; this is not a published free subscription.
Contact Chatora through the contact page for questions. Response times, 24/7 availability and dedicated support are not guaranteed.
