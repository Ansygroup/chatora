Inside the product

The workspace you get the second you sign up

Sign up to a clean workspace with no fake contacts or conversations. Import your own data, add a flow as a draft, and connect an official channel before anything is sent.

  • Real Supabase accounts with password sign-up; external sign-in providers are not configured yet
  • Your data is isolated per user by row-level security
  • Workspace data and flow drafts save to your account; outgoing messages are not recorded as sent
  • Arabic and English, right-to-left included
  • Broadcast audience previews can be filtered by tag or channel; sending is disabled until a provider is connected

Messaging channels

Everywhere your buyers already are

Telegram, web chat and the official Meta channels (WhatsApp Cloud API, Instagram, Messenger) can be connected with your own accounts. Meta channels need your own Meta app and its review; sending through a live Meta account is still being verified. TikTok, SMS and email are planned ideas.

Instagram

Comment-to-DM and Story workflows. Connect Instagram with your own Meta app; Meta's review applies.

WhatsApp

Opt-in support, catalogs and order messages. Use the official Cloud API with your own Meta app, or the unofficial OpenWA gateway (ban risk).

TikTok

Roadmap concept: comment follow-up and product-information flows. TikTok is not connected in this preview.

Messenger

Facebook Page messaging. Connect Messenger with your own Meta app; Meta's review applies.

Telegram

Bot and community workflows. Connect a Telegram bot from Settings.

SMS

Roadmap concept: opt-in SMS follow-up. No provider is connected; no open-rate claim is made.

Email

Roadmap concept: opt-in email follow-up. No email provider is connected.

Web chat

Website messaging. The web chat widget works from Settings.
