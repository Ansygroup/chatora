Understand the wallet model

Included credits are not the same as unlimited usage

Chatfuel's current documents describe a plan wallet: monthly credits can be spent on AI replies and Meta-billed WhatsApp use, with additional usage affected by the account's wallet settings. Chatora has no live AI or connected messaging channels, so there is no real usage or cost to compare yet.

  • The Business plan lists $20 in monthly credits; the yearly price is billed annually.
  • AI replies and certain WhatsApp costs are deducted from the wallet.
  • Meta rates vary by recipient country and message category.
  • Chatora's preview has no live AI, message charges or published subscription price.
