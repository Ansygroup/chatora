1. Published plan prices

On 28 October 2026, Chatfuel’s public pricing page listed Business at $20 month-to-month or $18 per month equivalent with annual billing ($216 billed yearly). The page lists $20 in monthly credits. It lists Agency S at $100 month-to-month or $90 per month equivalent yearly ($1,080 billed yearly), with $100 in monthly credits and three client companies. Regional terms and the exact treatment of credits should be confirmed on the vendor’s checkout and billing pages.

The pricing page offers a seven-day free trial; it does not list a permanent free plan. Its public plan page describes the included credits as AI credits, while Chatfuel’s billing documentation also describes wallet usage for AI and WhatsApp costs. Check the current terms for the account and charges you expect to run.

2. What the wallet covers

Chatfuel’s current billing documentation says wallet usage can include AI replies and WhatsApp message charges billed by Meta. The monthly credit is a budget contribution, not a promise of unlimited usage. Actual spend depends on the number and category of delivered messages, AI usage, any applicable free allowance, and the account’s billing settings.

3. What an all-in estimate needs

For a meaningful monthly estimate, collect your expected WhatsApp deliveries by message category and recipient country, your AI usage, the plan’s included monthly credit, any automatic-recharge settings, and applicable taxes. Meta’s own rate card charges by delivered message category and market, with a monthly free allowance for service messages; provider billing terms may be additional or presented differently. Compare the invoices—not just plan headlines.

Do not turn one sample AI response into a universal price-per-reply. Token use and model settings can vary, and wallet deductions depend on Chatfuel’s current billing rules. If the vendor’s dashboard offers a usage estimate, validate it against your actual invoice before scaling.

4. Where Chatfuel may fit

A low subscription headline is only one input. The current public pages may make Chatfuel worth evaluating when its listed channels, contact model, wallet or agency tools match your workflow.

  • Large contact stores. Chatfuel’s help documentation lists unlimited contacts on its plans; check the current plan terms and message-use economics for your own audience.
  • Teams comparing agency tools. The pricing page lists Agency S with three client companies and an agency dashboard. Compare the exact client limit and credit allowance to the number of brands you manage.
  • Teams that monitor usage. A wallet makes consumption visible, but it also means AI and messaging use can change the total. Review recharge settings and alerts before enabling automation.

5. Questions to check before buying

  1. Do monthly credits roll over? Check the current billing terms and account settings rather than assuming unused balances carry forward.
  2. What happens at the wallet threshold? Confirm whether automatic recharge is enabled, how it is triggered and how to pause it.
  3. How are WhatsApp message fees billed? Ask for the applicable category/market rate and a sample invoice; Meta’s public rate card may differ from a provider’s billing display.
  4. Is the trial a permanent free plan? No—the pricing page lists a seven-day trial, not a permanent free subscription tier.
