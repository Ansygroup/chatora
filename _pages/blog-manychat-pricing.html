1. The five tiers in the current snapshot

The pricing page lists the following contact allowances and USD prices. Monthly amounts below are the monthly equivalent when annual billing is selected; they are not month-to-month prices.

  • Free — $0: 25 contacts.
  • Essential — $14/month equivalent billed annually: 250 contacts.
  • Pro — $29/month equivalent billed annually: 2,500 contacts.
  • Business — $69/month equivalent billed annually: 7,500 contacts.
  • Advanced — $139/month equivalent billed annually: 25,000 contacts.

2. How to read annual and monthly prices

The amounts above are presented as annual-billing monthly equivalents. Check the checkout screen for the actual billing interval, total upfront charge, currency and any local tax before comparing them with a month-to-month quote.

Choose annual billing only if the term and contact allowance fit your expected usage. If your audience is growing quickly, compare the next tier and the vendor’s written overage or upgrade terms rather than assuming a specific per-contact charge.

4. Costs and terms to check

  • WhatsApp message fees. These are separate from a platform subscription and depend on message category, recipient market and provider billing. ManyChat’s guide and Meta’s rate card are not interchangeable; compare each source and your invoice.
  • Seats and channel access. User and channel availability can vary by tier. Check the current plan table for the number of users and channels your team needs.
  • Feature availability. AI tools and messaging channels may be limited to particular tiers. Verify the current inclusions in your country before upgrading.
  • Usage and overages. Do not assume a historical per-contact rate still applies. Ask ManyChat how it handles contacts above your allowance and when a tier change takes effect.
  • Currency and tax. USD examples may not be the amount shown to your billing country; taxes, exchange rates and local offers may change the final charge.

5. Questions before you switch

  1. Confirm what “contact” means. Read the current billing definition and check which contacts count toward your chosen allowance.
  2. Compare the next tier. Ask whether upgrading or the provider’s current overage terms are cheaper for your expected audience.
  3. Compare like billing periods. Keep annual-billed monthly equivalents separate from month-to-month prices and note any upfront commitment.
  4. Check the alternative’s actual availability. Chatora has not published subscription prices or enabled live channel messaging, so a valid cost or feature comparison is not currently possible.
