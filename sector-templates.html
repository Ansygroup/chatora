Sector templates

Ready starter flows for a restaurant, a clinic or salon, and an online shop. Copy or download the JSON, then paste it in Chatora under Settings, Templates and client mode. Flows import as drafts. Replace the [brackets] with your details before going live.

Triggers: /start opens a Telegram bot, keyword triggers match words inside a message. Templates never include contacts, keys or payment links.
