ManyChat alternatives in 2026: compare by use case

Five established alternatives, with current vendor snapshots and trade-offs. Chatora is listed transparently as a product preview—not a live ManyChat replacement: it has no published price, connected messaging channels or live AI replies.

Chatora Product preview · not a live alternative

Not published

Chatora currently offers an account-based product preview with editable flow drafts and CSV contact tools. No subscription price or paid plan is published, and no Instagram, WhatsApp or other messaging channel is connected. 2

ManyChat Established ecosystem

$0 – $139/mo*

ManyChat has a mature visual builder, an established community library and a published five-plan ladder. Its current pricing page lists 25 contacts on Free; WhatsApp and AI-powered conversations are on Pro and higher. Displayed prices depend on billing cycle and country.

  • Strengths: Established builder, 60+ templates listed on Free, public help centre and community.
  • Trade-offs: Active-contact limits and feature access vary by plan; regional prices and contact overages should be checked before buying.

3

Chatfuel Published agency options

$18+/mo*

Chatfuel lists Business at $18 per month equivalent when billed yearly ($20 monthly), with $20 in monthly wallet credits. Its Agency S listing includes three client companies and an agency dashboard. AI and Meta-billed WhatsApp usage draw from the wallet; actual charges depend on usage.

  • Strengths: Official Meta/WhatsApp provider status, listed channels, unlimited contacts and teammates, and an agency dashboard.
  • Trade-offs: No permanent free tier is listed; the free access is a 7-day trial. Wallet use and message fees add variable cost.
Chatora vs Chatfuel
4

Tidio Best for support workflows

$24.17/mo*

Tidio combines live chat, ticketing and automation for customer support. Its official pricing page lists Starter at $24.17 per month equivalent with annual billing for 100 billable conversations. Lyro AI and Flows have their own quotas, so compare total usage—not just the base plan.

  • Strengths: Live chat, ticketing, support workflows and a documented AI-agent option.
  • Trade-offs: Conversation and automation quotas apply; Lyro AI/Flows usage may be separate from the base plan.
5

ChatBot.com Best for website support and sales

$19/user/mo*

ChatBot.com’s Essential plan is listed at $19 per user per month with annual billing. It includes an AI agent, website chat, inbox and ticketing; the published Essential allowance is 10 AI resolutions per month. The official page also lists Messenger, WhatsApp and SMS (through Twilio), plus Shopify selling features.

  • Strengths: Website chat, support inbox and ticketing, AI agents, and listed commerce channels.
  • Trade-offs: Per-user pricing, limited included AI resolutions on Essential and no permanent free tier.
6

Botpress Best for custom conversational agents

Free – custom

Botpress offers a customizable agent platform with visual tooling and developer extensions. Its current pricing page lists a Free plan with 100 conversations, 3 seats and 3 agents; Plus starts at $150/month billed annually for 250 conversations, with Team and Enterprise tiers above it.

  • Strengths: Flexible agent-building tools, a published free tier and additional paid plans for larger workloads.
  • Trade-offs: Conversation limits and plan costs matter at scale; channel availability differs from social-first automation platforms.
