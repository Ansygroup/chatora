Integration roadmap · not live yet

Plan how your tools could connect

The services below are roadmap examples, not active integrations. The current preview has no native channel, store, payment, CRM, automation or public API connection.

No third-party connector is available in the current preview. Service names below indicate possible future integration targets only; no connection, sync, webhook or message delivery is enabled.

Commerce

Roadmap examples include Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Salla, Zid and Magento. Product, inventory, order and cart syncing are not available yet.

Payments

Roadmap examples include Stripe, Paymob, Fawry, Tap, PayTabs and PayPal. Checkout links, COD confirmation and refund-status sync are not available yet.

CRM & support

Roadmap examples include HubSpot, Zoho, Pipedrive, Freshdesk and Intercom. Lead sync, notes and support-ticket connections are not available yet.

Automation & data

Potential future targets include Zapier, Make, n8n, Google Sheets, Airtable, Notion and BigQuery. Triggers and conversation-event exports are not available yet.
