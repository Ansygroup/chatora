Rebuilt for 2026 — not patched

Every feature you need to sell in the DMs. None you don't.

Chatora’s product direction is AI-assisted conversation marketing: intent-aware replies, approved product information and human handoff. Telegram bots, the website chat widget and the official Meta channels (with your own Meta app) can be connected. Some ideas on this page are roadmap concepts and are marked as such.

Open the product preview Compare with ManyChat

Availability note: this page mixes live features with roadmap concepts and illustrative interface examples. Available today: accounts (password or Google), editable flow drafts, 45 editable starter drafts with generic placeholder steps, CSV contact tools, local flow tests, broadcasts, an inbox, Telegram, the website chat widget with a knowledge box, and the official Meta channels (WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger) through your own Meta app; sending through a live Meta account is still being verified. WhatsApp can also use an unofficial OpenWA gateway with a ban risk. The drafts are not production-tested. Commerce connectors and product analytics are not enabled. Figures and conversations shown are placeholders, not customer results.

01 · AI agent

Roadmap concept: AI grounded in your catalogue

A future version could use a product feed, FAQs and policy pages as reference material. Today there is no product lookup or live stock, price or shipping data.

  • Planned grounded replies: reference catalogue or FAQ content with price and claim guardrails; not available now
  • Planned intent routing: distinguish browsing, price questions and handoff requests; not available now
  • Planned human handoff: route selected requests to a person; not available now
  • Planned language support: Arabic and English interfaces are available; live AI replies and dialect matching are not enabled

02 · Automation

Flow automation: a roadmap concept

A future text prompt could help draft a workflow. The current preview lets you edit saved draft steps manually; it does not generate flows from prompts, activate triggers or execute automation.

  • Planned triggers include comments, DMs, Story mentions, ad clicks, keywords and webhooks; none is connected here
  • 45 editable starter drafts with generic placeholders; not production-tested or ready automations
  • Delay steps can be saved in drafts; timezone-aware quiet-hour rules are not implemented
  • Version history and one-click rollback are not available in the preview

03 · Unified inbox

Unified inbox: a future product direction

A shared inbox collects conversations from Telegram, web chat and the Meta channels you connect. TikTok, SMS and email are roadmap concepts.

  • The preview does not combine messages, orders, tags and notes across channels into one customer profile
  • Saved-reply libraries and AI-written drafts are not available; no AI provider is connected
  • Assignment rules, service-level automation and collision detection are not enabled
  • Team seats and plan limits are not published; this preview is scoped to the signed-in account

Interface concept only — no real contacts or conversations are shown.

04 · Broadcasts

Broadcast planning: segment, review and measure

A future broadcast workflow could target saved segments and selected send windows. The preview can show an audience from your contacts, but it cannot optimize timing, enforce frequency caps or send messages.

  • Per-contact send-time optimization is not available
  • A/B testing and automatic winner selection are not available
  • WhatsApp template review or approval is not available in the dashboard
  • Revenue attribution per broadcast is not available

05 · Commerce

Roadmap concept: connect product questions to checkout

A future commerce integration could connect product, catalogue and checkout data. No Shopify, WooCommerce, Stripe or other commerce connector is enabled in this preview; order attribution is unavailable.

  • Live stock and product-variant checks are not available
  • Automated abandoned-cart recovery and unique expiring links are not available
  • COD and regional payment integrations are not available
  • Post-purchase tracking, review requests and reorder nudges are not enabled

06 · Analytics

Analytics roadmap: connect conversations to outcomes

Analytics do not connect order events, so Chatora cannot attribute revenue, calculate ticket deflection or score AI quality.

  • Revenue attribution by flow, agent, keyword, channel or campaign is not available
  • Automated ticket-resolution and deflection reports are not available
  • AI quality scorecards and hallucination flags are not available
  • Exports to Sheets, BigQuery or BI tools via API are not available

07 · Trust & security

Verify security controls before relying on them

This page is not a security certification or service-level agreement. Channels you connect send messages through their providers; review the privacy notice before submitting data.

Official API status

The product direction favors documented, approved APIs. No external channel integration or Meta partner approval has been verified for this preview.

Privacy and data residency

A verified hosting-region list, signed DPA, encryption-at-rest specification and security certifications are not published; see the legal notice.

Availability commitments

No 99.99% uptime target, multi-region redundancy or public status page has been verified or promised.

AI data handling

No AI provider is connected and external AI processing is not enabled. Do not submit sensitive data; terms for any future provider are not established here.

Audit logs and SSO

Role-based permissions, immutable audit logs and SSO are not available in this preview; no team-level permission model is advertised.
