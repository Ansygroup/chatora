ManyChat vs Chatfuel in 2026: plan prices, wallet billing and what to verify

ManyChat uses contact-based plan tiers; Chatfuel combines a subscription with monthly credits and wallet usage. This dated comparison separates those public figures from message fees and usage that depend on your account. Chatora is included only as a product-preview status, not as a live priced competitor.

1. Three published billing models

The plan pages show different billing units; they do not prove that the tools include the same features or produce the same final bill:

  • ManyChat — contact tiers. Its USD page checked 28 October lists Free at 25 contacts, Essential at 250, Pro at 2,500, Business at 7,500 and Advanced at 25,000. Annual-billing monthly equivalents are $14, $29, $69 and $139 for the paid tiers.
  • Chatfuel — subscription plus credits and wallet usage. Business is listed at $20 month-to-month or $18/month equivalent billed yearly, with $20 in monthly credits. Agency S is listed at $100 monthly or $90/month equivalent yearly, with $100 in credits and three client companies. Check how the wallet applies to your use.
  • Chatora — product preview. There is no published subscription price, connected messaging channel or live AI reply. It cannot be assigned a comparable plan or message cost today.

For an all-in monthly estimate, separate the subscription from usage credits, delivered WhatsApp messages, taxes and any provider-specific fees. Do not infer that a fee is passed through at cost—or that a markup applies—without the vendor’s current billing terms.

3. Where each platform may fit

A useful comparison gives each product its own strengths and limits:

  • ManyChat — established ecosystem. It has a mature builder, community resources and a public partner programme. Check whether its current channel, AI and support features fit your plan.
  • Chatfuel — wallet and agency options. Its public pages list WhatsApp provider status, unlimited contacts in help documentation and an Agency S option. The wallet and actual usage need review.
  • Chatora — product preview. It offers Arabic and English interface text, editable starter drafts and CSV tools. It is not a live messaging alternative and has no published subscription price.

4. Questions before switching

  1. What data can you export? Save contact records, flow definitions and message templates before changing a provider; verify each platform’s export tools.
  2. Which channel permissions are required? Check official API access, account ownership and approval requirements before connecting a production account.
  3. Which features will your team lose or gain? Compare template libraries, agency workflows, integrations, language support and help commitments.
  4. What is the actual message price? Ask for the applicable category/market rate and a sample invoice; Meta’s card and a provider’s billing display may differ.

5. Verdict by use case

  • Need live social messaging now: evaluate providers with currently available, officially supported channels and confirm plan limits.
  • Need a large contact store or wallet model: compare Chatfuel’s published plan, credits and account-level usage with your expected message mix.
  • Need a mature template ecosystem: ManyChat may be a fit; check the current local offer and feature tier.
  • Considering Chatora: the current preview is not production messaging software. Review the availability page; no price or savings comparison is published.

Before buying, record your contact count, channel list, expected message categories, AI usage and billing country. Ask each vendor for the written terms and compare the same period and feature set.

Frequently asked, briefly

Is Chatfuel cheaper than ManyChat?

The public subscription prices differ, but the total depends on contact limits, feature access, monthly credits, wallet deductions, Meta message charges, taxes and billing period. A subscription-only comparison is not an all-in answer.

Can I compare Chatora’s price with them?

No. Chatora has no published subscription price or live messaging channel, so there is no equivalent plan or usage total to compare.

Do contacts automatically transfer when I switch?

Do not assume that they do. Export and back up the records available from your current provider and verify the channel’s ownership and transfer rules before disconnecting it.

Where can I see current Chatora availability?

The pricing page states what is and is not enabled in the current preview. It does not publish a paid plan or promise live channel messaging.

Check the published terms, not an assumed total

Review each vendor’s current price and usage rules. Chatora’s page shows its preview status; it cannot calculate an all-in comparison yet.
