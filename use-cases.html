Four illustrative workflow concepts

Whatever you sell, the buyer starts with a question

Illustrative workflow ideas for shops, creators, agencies and clinics—not live automations, customer case studies or measured outcomes.

Availability: the use cases below are illustrative workflow concepts, not customer case studies. The preview supports editable drafts, CSV contact tools and audience previews only; it has no live channel connections, AI replies or measured performance results.

01 · Online shops

Plan after-hours support for cart questions

A future cart-support workflow could route questions such as "is it in stock?", "does it fit?", or "can I pay on delivery?" to approved product and policy information. That workflow is not connected in the current preview.

  • Concept: check stock and variants through a future commerce integration (not available)
  • Concept: collect a cash-on-delivery confirmation in a draft flow
  • Revenue attribution is not available in the current preview
